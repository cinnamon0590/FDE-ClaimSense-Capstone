Four AI agents read the file, check cover against the actual policy clauses they retrieved, assess fraud, injury and total-loss risk, and draft a decision. Every data request goes through what we call the governed door: plain code that decides who may see which claim, calculates dates and amounts, and writes an audit row for every call, including refusals. The model never decides access or money. Below: what your team inherits. A runbook whose procedures we actually executed on the live system, a dashboard with an alarm that emails on-call, and the test suite that acts as the contract: 170 claims re-run on every change, and a release that fails a single attack does not ship.
