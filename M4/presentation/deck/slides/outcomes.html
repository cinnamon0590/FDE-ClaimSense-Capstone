Four numbers, and each is measured, not projected. First, speed. Today a first decision takes a median of 3.2 hours, about 55 minutes of it hands-on work across claims, policy admin and the document store. ClaimSense returns a first decision in 13 seconds for 95 percent of claims. Second, accuracy. The rule table you use today got 25 percent of our test claims right. ClaimSense got all 150 right, three runs in a row with identical answers. Because we tuned on those 150, we also wrote 20 new claims it had never seen, locked the expected answers before running, and ran them once: 19 of 20. Third, fraud. The rule table caught none of the fraud files. ClaimSense routed all 12 to the investigators and sent zero honest claims there by mistake, which is what SIU asked for. Fourth, cost: about two and a half cents of model time per claim. If asked about the one held-out miss: the decision was right, but a UK passport number was not masked before reaching the model. It was fixed the same day and is covered by a test. I cover it on the safety slide.
